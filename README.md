# Supplementary Code S1

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23104508.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23104508)

Code and computed values for the review:

**Photothermal Nanomaterials and Thermo-Sensitive Genic Male Sterility in Rice:
A Quantitative Feasibility Analysis of Microclimate Engineering for Climate-Resilient
Hybrid Breeding**

## DOI

Zenodo DOI supplied for this archive: [10.5281/zenodo.23104508](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23104508)

This archive contains everything needed to reproduce, from scratch, every number
labelled *computed* or *modelled* in the manuscript and every one of its ten figures.
No experimental data were generated by the review; all empirical values quoted in
the tables are taken from the cited primary literature and are attributed there.

## Reproducing

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
python run_all.py
```

Runtime is under a minute on an ordinary laptop. Figure output is deterministic:
the one stochastic component, the simulated multi-environment trial of Fig. 10,
is seeded (`numpy.random.default_rng(7)` in `simulate_met`, and
`default_rng(20260401)` in `figs3.py`), so repeated runs are bit-identical.

## Scope and limits of the models

`dT_steady` implements the linearised organ energy balance in its molar form
(Campbell & Norman 1998; Jones 2014; Monteith & Unsworth 2013). Three choices are
made deliberately in the direction most favourable to the intervention under test:
photothermal conversion efficiency is set to unity, interception of the incident
flux is set to unity, and the extra latent heat loss induced by warming is set to
zero. Every temperature increment reported is therefore an **upper bound**.

`water_offset` is a saturating curve fitted to the qualitative field literature on
deep-water protection of booting panicles; it is a model, not a measured dataset,
and is labelled as such in the manuscript and in `computed_values.csv`.

`simulate_met` generates **synthetic** data on the logit scale. It exists to size
future experiments and to illustrate the behaviour of the stability statistics. No
panel of Fig. 10 reports an observation from any real sterile line or any real
nanomaterial treatment.

`solar_spectrum` in `figs2.py` is an analytic stand-in for the AM1.5G reference
spectrum (a 5778 K Planck curve attenuated by a Rayleigh term and the principal
water-vapour, oxygen and carbon-dioxide bands), normalised to 1000 W m-2. Its share
above 700 nm is 0.532, against the tabulated value of approximately 0.52 (ASTM
G173-03). It is used for illustration in Fig. 4; the energy-balance calculations use
the tabulated fraction of 0.52 directly.
